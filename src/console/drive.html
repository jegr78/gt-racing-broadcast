Which setup?

  • Nothing else receives your telemetry: run the command as shown.
  • A dashboard app on your phone: run the sender on a second device on the same network (PC, laptop, tablet). Two programs cannot share one device's telemetry port.
  • SimHub on the same PC: add --forward 127.0.0.1:PORT and point SimHub at that port with its own heartbeat off.
  • GT Race Engineer forwards to a port: add --listen PORT; the sender then sends no heartbeat.

Allow inbound UDP 33740 in the device's firewall: the console answers from a port of its own. On iOS keep a-Shell in front with the screen on and the device charging. The driver guide in the GT Racing Broadcast wiki covers every platform.
